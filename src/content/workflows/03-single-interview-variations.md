This is a single street interview. Below is a prompt built for a specific street interview format. My format features different questions and topics, but a similar q/a pattern and I want to work with you to build the same 5X video variations out of my footage.

Here's a street interview in my matchmaker format: I stop one person, run a fixed set of dating questions, and pitch them to the audience. One interview, one video, five versions of it. Tell me what's in there before you cut anything.

FORMAT
A repeatable dating-matchmaker Q&A. Same question script every time. The edit tightens it into a fast, warm, funny clip that opens on "Are you single?" and closes by pitching the person to the audience. Their personality plus my playful reframes carry it.

THE QUESTION ARC
1. Open: "Are you single?"
2. "I want to help you find a very cool boyfriend / girlfriend."
3. "Why are you single?"
4. "What's your type?" then my playful reframe
5. "What's your ideal first date?"
6. "What do you bring into a relationship?"
7. Optional: do they need to be local, or open to long distance
8. Close: "If you're a cool guy / girl who wants to date this person, slide into their DMs."

The interview may run differently than this. Follow what's in the footage.

PLAN FIRST
Give me five versions and tell me what each one opens on before you build:

A, one version. Cold open on the opening question and their reaction. The signature.
B, three versions. Their best funny, surprising or spicy lines, each cold-opening on a different one for 3 to 7 seconds, then cutting back to "Are you single?" and running the arc. Pick the three most distinct so the versions feel genuinely different.
C, one version. Start from a middle question, run through to the DM line, then cut back to the open.

For each one:
- the beat it opens on, quoted from what was actually said
- the title card text you would put on the cold open
- roughly how long it runs

If there genuinely aren't three distinct funny moments in there, say so and give me the best available rather than forcing weak hooks.

Don't build anything until I've picked from the list.

THEN BUILD
Vertical, my default caption style. No B-roll, no music. For the title card on the cold open - use the basic title card with white background. The duration of this title card should match the cold open it sits on, OR be 6s if that version doesn't have one.

If I don't have a default caption style, tell me to go to the brand guide from the home screen and save a default caption style, then come back here to add it to the plan.

Narrative-driven pacing. Keep the full arc. Length is emergent, roughly 45 to 80 seconds, never a target, never cut for time. Trim only genuine dead air over about three seconds, filler, false starts, repeated takes, and energy dips. Keep the banter, the natural pauses, and the funny beats.

Cut off-topic tangents, anyone else or behind-camera chatter, and long walking with no talking.

DO NOT
Change the question order after the chosen opener. Cut the open or the matchmaker close. Lose my playful reframes, they are the charm.

If I upload a batch of multiple interviews or guests, tell me to go back to the Clik landing page clik.vision and copy the compilation flow prompt.
