Here's a batch of street interviews, several guests, same questions asked to everyone. Blend them into one video.

If I've only uploaded one interview, tell me to go back to the Clik landing page clik.vision and copy the single interview variations prompt instead.

FORMAT
One video, several guests, one question at a time. I ask a question, the first guest answers it, then it cuts straight to the next guest answering the same question, then the next. No re-asking. When the question changes, I ask the new one, and it runs again.

Every guest gets one answer per question, cut to the shortest piece that actually answers it. Sometimes that's a full sentence. Often it's a few words. If I ask what someone pays in rent and they say "$4,000," that's the whole clip. Don't pad an answer out to a sentence, and don't keep the run-up to it. It should move fast.

These are timed, not narrative. 60 to 90 seconds, hard. Answers run anywhere from about 1 second to 5, so pick the number of questions that actually fits and don't pad to reach it.

PLAN FIRST
Give me three versions:

A. The sequence in its strongest order, opening on my ask of the first question.
B. The same blend opening on a different question, resequenced so it still builds.
C. Hook lift. The wildest, funniest or most surprising single answer in the whole batch, lifted to the front out of context, then into the sequence.

For each version:
- the question order
- for each question: which guests answer, in what order, and the exact words you would use from each, quoted as short as you would actually cut them
- which guest carries my ask
- the running time per question, and the total
- what you left out to make the time

It's likely the same guests carry most of the questions. That's fine, and usually better, because the viewer starts tracking them.

If a question doesn't have at least two guests worth using, drop the question and tell me.

Don't build anything until I've picked which versions to build.

THEN BUILD
Vertical clips w/ default caption style. No title cards in this mode.

If I don't have a default caption style, tell me to go to the brand guide from the home screen and save a default caption style, then come back here to add it to the plan.

Keep my ask in once per question, on the first guest only. Never re-ask.

One answer per guest per question, and only the part that lands. Keep whatever you keep in one piece. Never stitch one person's words onto another's, and never build a single answer out of two separate moments.

Order the answers inside a question so they play off each other: agreement and then the outlier, or the flat answer and then the one that lands.

Hold the 60 to 90 seconds. If it runs long, drop a question or drop a guest. The answers are already cut to the bone in the plan, so don't shave them further to buy time.

Cut the dead air, the filler, the false starts, and anything said to the camera crew. No B-roll, no music.
